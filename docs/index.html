Shipping v0.1.1

Court clerk support that respects sealed-record boundaries.

CivicCourt helps municipal court clerks answer procedure questions with citations, draft forms, persist optional local workpapers, search public sample records, and prepare hearing checklists.

For Clerks

Draft procedure answers, forms, and hearing prep checklists that a clerk verifies before use.

For IT

FastAPI runtime pinned to civiccore==0.3.0, optional persistence with CIVICCOURT_WORKPAPER_DB_URL, deterministic tests, and no court CMS writes.

For Safety

Restricted records are hidden in the sample search contract; sealed and juvenile matters are not exposed.

Architecture

Boundaries

CivicCourt v0.1.1 does not replace court case management, issue warrants, file documents, decide cases, provide legal advice, call live LLMs, or integrate with court CMS systems.
