# CivicCourt Reconciliation

The canonical catalog defines CivicCourt as court and clerk document support: forms generation, procedure Q&A over court rules and municipal code, court forms, form generation from case data, and procedural Q&A with citations.

v0.1.0 intentionally ships deterministic foundations only. It does not replace court case-management systems, issue warrants, file documents, decide cases, provide legal advice, expose sealed or juvenile records, or connect to court CMS systems.
